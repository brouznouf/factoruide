# Factoruide Profile

What changes for players in each version of this addon, shown on its CurseForge and Wago pages.
Same rules as the app's CHANGELOG.md: one short line per change, what the player sees. New lines go
under Unreleased; `scripts/release.sh` gives them the release's version.

## Unreleased

## 0.17.0 (2026-11-09)

- The objective guide's level warning in every language of the game.

## 0.16.0 (2026-11-07)

- Lighter: the guides made in the desktop app now count in the Guide addon's memory, not this one's, and your reputations and spells are no longer read again every minute.

## 0.15.0 (2026-11-01)

- Texts of the death skip steps, in every language.
- Texts of the group guide player choice, in every language.
- The hearthstone's inn is found whatever the game's language (the guide no longer sends you back to your starting place).
- No more error when logging out before the realm name is known.

## 0.14.0 (2026-10-20)

- In the game's addon list, the Factoruide addons are under Quests, with their own icon, Guide and Data grouped under Profile.
- Translations of Factoruide Guide's new step labels (vendor, and steps of a kind it does not know yet).

## 0.13.0 (2026-10-19)

- First version on CurseForge and Wago: your character and what the game shows, recorded for the Factoruide app.
